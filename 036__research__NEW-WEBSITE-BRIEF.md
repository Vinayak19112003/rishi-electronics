# New website build brief

Purpose: turn local repair enquiries into a clear staff-confirmed request, phone call or WhatsApp conversation.

## Source of truth

Load contact/address/hours from business.json and categories from services.json. Preserve all nine services, including RO. Unknown values are null; do not fill them with invented prices, ratings, warranty promises or experience counts. Tamil labels and short descriptions are suggested drafts.

## Suggested pages

Home; /services; one /services/<suggested_page_slug> per category; /about; /book-repair; /contact; /privacy; /terms. Content and terms must reflect confirmed operations. No shop checkout or customer dashboard is required by the collected business data.

## Homepage composition

Use a clear local headline, workshop imagery, a service selector, phone/book actions, nine service cards, a short repair process based on owner input, real shop information, verified trust evidence and a full contact block. Persistent mobile call and WhatsApp actions should remain easy to access without covering content.

## Booking

The time is a preference, pending confirmation. Use Asia/Kolkata date calculations. Normalize Indian phone numbers. Include locality/address only for confirmed home-visit workflows. Disable duplicate submissions while pending; show failure and fallback contact options. Display success only after provider acceptance and distinguish that from a confirmed appointment. Configure a new form destination and verify delivery in an approved launch test.

## Visual direction

Retain brand identity and use a consistent restrained palette. Use real repair/workshop photos when possible. Replace undersized home-theater, inverter and RO pictures for larger displays. Optimize separate delivery copies; keep original assets. Prefer a simple hero over multiple heavy auto-playing images. Support Tamil/English and reduced motion if chosen.

## Search and accessibility

Choose one canonical domain; redirect aliases when authorized. Add unique titles/descriptions, sitemap, crawl directives, social preview and business/service structured data containing confirmed facts. Add accessible navigation and dialogs, visible focus, labels, useful error messages and keyboard operation. Do not publish unverified rating schema.

## Launch acceptance

Verify nine service routes and selector options; phone/WhatsApp/maps links; consistent address/hours; form delivery and error behavior; local date handling; mobile navigation; keyboard focus and dialog close; reduced motion; image sizing; domain aliases; metadata and sitemap. Replace placeholders and review claims before publishing. The research package is not a live deployed website.
